  • Introducing yourself

    • There are two ways to address people in German

      1. Friends / children are addressed with du
      2. Adults you don't know with Sie
        • Sie is always used with a capital "S"
        • Sie is used with
          • Someone older
          • Someone with higher rank
          • To show respect
      Familiar (du) Unfamiliar (sie) Remarks Meaning
