#+TITLE: About

I am a software engineer by profession.

I believe writing software is a lot more than just churning out code. Software is incarnation of a
person's thoughts. The better one know oneself, clearer (s)he can think, better (s)he can code. I
see getting better at life as a way of getting better at writing good software; and vice-versa.

My life more or less revolves around computers. I enjoy thinking and thought exercises; writing
software is one of my favorites.

I am open to take freelance work. I have most experience in Javascript/Typescript (both frontend and
backend); but can also comfortably work with Python, Rust, Haskell and Common Lisp. I am happiest
with Common Lisp and Haskell.
