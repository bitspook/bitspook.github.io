    E, e eh egg
    I, i ee feet

  • Dipthongs - Dipthonge

    Dipthongs mean Gliding vowels which are a combination of two adjacent vowel sounds in the same syllable.

    Character Sound Example
    au ow aus (from)
    ei aye  
    ai aye  
    eu oy europa
    äu oy  
    ie ee
